Kaijie Jiang

I am a Ph.D. student in cryptography at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Xiaoyun Wang. My research interests are mainly in post-quantum cryptography, lattice-based cryptography, lattice isomorphism problems, and threshold signatures.

I have also visited the National University of Singapore, where I am hosted by Prof. Divesh Aggarwal. Recently, I have been working on the theory and algorithms of the lattice isomorphism problem, as well as adaptively secure lattice-based threshold signatures.
